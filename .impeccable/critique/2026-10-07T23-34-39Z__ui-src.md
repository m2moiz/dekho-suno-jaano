## Priority issues
1. [P1] Urdu turns sit a page-width away from their margin. Nameplate at x about 232, the Urdu line starts at x about 1124 in the reader and 1130 in Review, so every turn costs a full-width eye jump between who and the first word. Fix: give right-to-left turns a narrower measure (40 to 45ch) so their start edge sits near the margin, or repeat a slim nameplate at the line's start; keep time and tick on the left. /impeccable layout. Seen in reader-urdu-1440x900-light.png, reader-urdu-1440x900-dark.png, review-urdu-1440x900-light.png, review-urdu-1440x900-dark.png.
2. [P1] Phone Review stacks two gold targets: "Checked, next" sits just above the rail's gold play button, and Back above play. One-handed, a thumb aimed at play marks a sentence checked, and checked sentences become answer-key truth. It also breaks the One Gold Rule. Fix: in phone Review drop or outline the rail's play (the card already has Play again) and clear 12 px or more above the rail. /impeccable adapt. Seen in review-390x844-light.png, review-390x844-dark.png, review-urdu-390x844-dark.png, review-flags-390x844-light.png.
3. [P1] Tab inside the Review box plays and pauses instead of moving focus, and nothing on the box says how to leave. Option+Tab does exit (keymap.ts) and the key sheet's notes say so (keys.ts), but the box carries no aria-describedby (none in features/review), so a keyboard or screen-reader user meets what behaves as a trap (WCAG 2.1.2 asks that a non-standard exit be announced). Fix: describe the box ("Tab plays. Option+Tab moves on.") and add Option+Tab to the footer line. /impeccable harden. Seen in review-1440x900-light.png (footer "Tab play").
4. [P2] "4 unsure" counts words it does not mark: the tint is off until the count is pressed (UnsureNav.tsx), and the count looks like a static label. Fix: tint on by default, or style the count as a toggle with a visible pressed state. /impeccable clarify. Seen in reader-1440x900-light.png, reader-urdu-1440x900-dark.png, menu-1440x900-light.png.
5. [P2] The laptop desk has no pointer path and no speaker map: no Checked button, no speaker chips; "Ctrl 1 to 9 speaker" in the footer works only while names are "Speaker n" and turns into recall once speakers are renamed. Fix: speaker chips with their key in the margin, and a quiet "Checked, next" ghost button under the box. /impeccable clarify. Seen in review-1440x900-light.png, review-1440x900-dark.png, review-urdu-1440x900-light.png.

## Questions to consider
- If Urdu is first-class, should the margin follow the script's start edge, even at the cost of a constant grid?
- A checked sentence becomes ground truth for scoring models: should Enter or a tap count as checked before the sentence has played through once?
- Reading is the most frequent job and the reader is now the plainest screen: what would its margin carry (review marks, corrections, flags) so the hashiya shows before anyone presses R?
