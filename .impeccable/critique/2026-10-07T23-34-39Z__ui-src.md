Nameplate at x about 232, the Urdu line starts at x about 1124 in the reader and 1130 in Review, so every turn costs a full-width eye jump between who and the first word. 2. [P1] Phone Review stacks two gold targets: "Checked, next" sits just above the rail's gold play button, and Back above play. One-handed, a thumb aimed at play marks a sentence checked, and checked sentences become answer-key truth. It also breaks the One Gold Rule. Fix: in phone Review drop or outline the rail's play (the card already has Play again) and clear 12 px or more above the rail. /impeccable adapt. Seen in review-390x844-light.png, review-390x844-dark.png, review-urdu-390x844-dark.png, review-flags-390x844-light.png.
3. [P1] Tab inside the Review box plays and pauses instead of moving focus, and nothing on the box says how to leave. Fix: describe the box ("Tab plays. Option+Tab moves on.") and add Option+Tab to the footer line. /impeccable harden. Seen in review-1440x900-light.png (footer "Tab play").
4. [P2] "4 unsure" counts words it does not mark: the tint is off until the count is pressed (UnsureNav.tsx), and the count looks like a static label. Fix: tint on by default, or style the count as a toggle with a visible pressed state. /impeccable clarify. Seen in reader-1440x900-light.png, reader-urdu-1440x900-dark.png, menu-1440x900-light.png.

## Questions to consider
- If Urdu is first-class, should the margin follow the script's start edge, even at the cost of a constant grid?
- A checked sentence becomes ground truth for scoring models: should Enter or a tap count as checked before the sentence has played through once?
- Reading is the most frequent job and the reader is now the plainest screen: what would its margin carry (review marks, corrections, flags) so the hashiya shows before anyone presses R?
